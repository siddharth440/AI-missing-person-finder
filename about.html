System Overview & Ethics

About FindSafe AI

Connecting information. Finding possibilities. Bringing people home.

The Challenge

Missing person investigations often involve massive quantities of fragmented, unorganized information scattered across community boards, social media feeds, and police filings. Critical early hours are lost to manual data triage.

The Solution

FindSafe AI provides a centralized, browser-native platform that aggregates case dossiers, logs community sightings, plots geospatial incident maps, and runs visual similarity scoring to prioritize potential leads.

Key Platform Advantages

Fast Lead Triage

Structured fields and vector comparison narrow down thousands of images into actionable candidate leads.

Geospatial Intelligence

Client-side OpenStreetMap integration visualizes last-seen coordinates and sighting density clusters.

Zero-Backend Portability

Runs 100% client-side using browser LocalStorage. Requires no servers, databases, or API keys to deploy directly on GitHub Pages.

Privacy-Conscious Architecture

Supports anonymous tip submission and avoids collecting unnecessary personal telemetry.

Responsible AI & Educational Disclaimer

FindSafe AI is designed strictly as an educational prototype and hackathon concept. The photo similarity engine implemented in this system simulates feature extraction and vector matrix comparison.

Mandatory Notice: AI-generated match percentages do NOT constitute biometric legal identification or proof of identity. All candidate matches generated by this platform are potential leads intended solely for manual verification by qualified human investigators and law enforcement personnel.
