How FindSafe AI Operates

A structured 5-step methodology designed to organize information and accelerate critical leads.

1

Register Case

Families or authorized reporters register detailed missing-person profiles with photographs.

2

Upload Information

Upload clothing descriptions, physical marks, last-known GPS coordinates, and contact details.

3

AI-Assisted Match

Similarity algorithms evaluate incoming photo sightings against active case records.

4

Verify Potential Leads

High-confidence potential matches are flagged for manual verification by emergency personnel.

5

Resolve & Reunite

Case records are updated upon safe location confirmation by authorized authorities.
